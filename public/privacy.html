Privacy Policy

Iron Log · last updated 28 July 2026

Iron Log does not collect, send or store any personal data.

You do not make an account. There is no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We have no server that receives your data.

What the app stores, and where

The app writes all the data that you enter to storage on your device. The data stays there. This includes workouts, sets, weights, reps, personal records, programmes, bodyweight readings, height, age and your settings.

We cannot see any of this data. No copy of it exists in a different location.

What leaves your device

No data that you enter leaves your device.

The purchase is the only network activity. If you buy the lifetime unlock, Google Play Billing does all of the transaction. Google processes the payment. Google then tells the app if you own the product. We never receive or use your payment details. The transaction does not include workout data. Google's Privacy Policy covers how Google uses the transaction data.

Permissions

The app does not ask for runtime permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or health data.

Deleting your data

Your data is only on your device. When you uninstall Iron Log, this removes your data. You can also delete the data at any time in the app settings of your device. We have no data to delete for you, because we never had your data.

Note: This removal is permanent. There is no cloud copy, so you cannot recover data that you remove in this way.

Children

Iron Log is not for children under 13. The app collects no data from any person, and this includes children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this address. We will also change the date above.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, open an issue on the GitHub repository of the project.
