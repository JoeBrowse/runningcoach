# Mass-market / beginner-friendly running apps with coaching (state as of 2026-11-25)

Scope: Nike Run Club (NRC), adidas Running (ex-Runtastic), ASICS Runkeeper, MapMyRun (Outside), Peloton App (running), NHS Couch to 5K, Zwift Run / Zwift Companion, Openfit, Zen Labs C25K / Couch-to-5K-style apps.

Source-quality note (read first): Official 2026 price pages were hard to obtain. Many 2026 price figures come from affiliate/comparison blogs (runandgrow.com, runifyapp.com, motera.app, subscription trackers) that contradict each other. Where that is the case it is flagged. Items marked "[UNVERIFIED - pre-2026 knowledge]" in the feature matrix are from the researcher's background knowledge and were not confirmed by a 2026 source in this session; the report writer should treat them as provisional (they are also listed under Gaps).

## Current price, free tier, what made each app popular, and what it does

### Takeaway
The mass-market tier is split between fully free, content-led apps (Nike Run Club, NHS Couch to 5K, Zwift running) and cheap freemium apps whose coaching plans sit behind a roughly $30-50/yr paywall (Runkeeper Go, adidas Running Premium, MapMyRun MVP, Zen Labs C25K). Peloton is the expensive outlier at about $15.99/mo. Their popularity comes from audio-guided runs (NRC Guided Runs, NHS celebrity coaches, adidas Story Running) and from being free and easy for beginners, not from adaptive, data-driven training.

### Cited Findings

**Nike Run Club (Nike)**
- Still completely free in 2026: no subscription tier, no in-app ads, no upsells. It has been free since launch and had not moved to a paid model as of 2026. — [The Sunrise Digest NRC review 2026](https://thesunrisedigest.com/move/nike-run-club-review-2026/)
- Core features: GPS run tracking, plans built by "NRC Coach" toward 5K, 10K, half or full marathon, and a library of audio-coached Guided Runs (mostly led by Coach Chris Bennett, with guests such as Eliud Kipchoge and Sanya Richards-Ross). Guided Runs cue pacing, form and breathing. — [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/move/nike-run-club-review-2026/)
- Guided Runs are the headline feature and the main reason runners praise the app. The reviewer calls the plans "genuinely excellent" for a free app but less adaptive than Runna because they do not adjust week to week. — [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/move/nike-run-club-review-2026/)
- Tom's Guide: detailed training plans "can coach you up to half marathon distance". Plans and Guided Runs are offered only in some countries. — [Tom's Guide NRC review](https://www.tomsguide.com/uk/reviews/nike-run-club-review); [motera.app NRC 2026](https://www.motera.app/nike-run-club). Note the conflict: Sunrise Digest says plans go up to the full marathon.
- Recent changes: v7.76 (March 2026) added a Guided Runs search bar. v7.79.4 (around 9 Nov 2026) lets you repeat a Guided Run from your activity, and average pace now uses total time and distance. Live run sharing (real-time location sharing with friends and family) and weather-based pre-run tips date from 2024-25. In-app race search and sign-up arrived in October 2025. — [Nike newsroom](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-run-club-app-new-features); [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/move/nike-run-club-review-2026/); [motera.app](https://www.motera.app/nike-run-club)
- Historical: Nike marketed "personalized workouts, adaptive coaching" when NRC Coach launched (2016-era article). — [Marketing Dive / Mobile Marketer](https://www.marketingdive.com/ex/mobilemarketer/cms/sectors/sports/23490.html)
- Watch and ecosystem: works well with Apple Watch and sends runs to Apple Health and Strava. There is no deep Garmin, COROS or Polar integration, so Garmin and COROS owners usually record on their own watch and use NRC only for Guided Runs. — [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/move/nike-run-club-review-2026/)
- Garmin runs do sync into NRC, but (as of about 2024) they could not be counted toward an NRC plan. Users re-created NRC workouts by hand in Garmin Connect. — [Garmin Forums thread](https://forums.garmin.com/apps-software/mobile-apps-web/f/garmin-connect-web/371627/nike-run-club-training-plan)
- Wear OS: a 2026 review site says a dedicated NRC Wear OS app exists. This is a low-authority source. — [motera.app](https://www.motera.app/nike-run-club)
- Removals: the only documented removal is from 30 April 2018, when Nike ended support for legacy Nike+ wearables and NRC 4.x and earlier. No 2023-2026 removal of plans was found. — [Wikipedia: Nike+ FuelBand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nike%2B_FuelBand); search summary of [Garmin forums / Tom's Guide results](https://www.tomsguide.com/uk/reviews/nike-run-club-review)
- Complaints: plans are less adaptive, social features are thin (a follower feed with kudos that is much quieter than Strava), hardware integration is limited, and some users dislike the heavy Nike branding (swoosh at the start of Guided Runs). — [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/move/nike-run-club-review-2026/)

**adidas Running (formerly Runtastic)**

### Inferences
- NRC is the anchor of the free tier because a high-production audio coach costs nothing. NHS C25K plays the same role for absolute beginners in the UK. Both win on content and motivation rather than data or adaptivity.
- The freemium apps (adidas, Runkeeper, MapMyRun) look like legacy products under cost-cutting owners: adidas laid off the whole Runtastic team and shut adidas Training, ASICS is criticised for slow development, and MapMyFitness was sold for about $8M against a $150M purchase. Expect little feature innovation, and possible further shutdowns.
- Peloton is the only mass-market brand in this set putting AI into running (Pace Targets, Run Analysis). Its strongest features appear tied to its expensive treadmill hardware.
- Zwift Plus's "adaptive coach" is aimed mainly at indoor cycling, but it signals that adaptive coaching is spreading to platform subscriptions.

### Gaps

## Top complaints and praise, unique features, and features runners would miss

### Takeaway
Praise centres on free audio coaching and motivation (NRC Guided Runs, NHS celebrity coaches, Zwift's gamified indoor runs). Complaints centre on plans that are not adaptive, paywalls (Runkeeper Go, Zen Labs Unlimited, adidas Premium), weak watch integration (NRC with Garmin and COROS, NHS with no watch app) and neglect after ownership changes or layoffs.

### Cited Findings
- NRC praise: free, ad-free, clean UI, a credentialed coach and strong coaching content. NRC complaints: plans that do not adapt, thin social features, limited Garmin, COROS and Polar integration, and heavy branding. — [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/move/nike-run-club-review-2026/)
- NRC with Garmin: runs from Garmin import, but there is no structured-workout push, so plan workouts have to be re-created by hand in Garmin Connect. — [Garmin Forums](https://forums.garmin.com/apps-software/mobile-apps-web/f/garmin-connect-web/371627/nike-run-club-training-plan)
- Runkeeper: valued as cheap ($39.99/yr vs Strava's $79.99/yr). Criticised for its paywall, slow development and audio that is weaker than NRC's. — [motera.app](https://www.motera.app/runkeeper-app); [runandgrow](https://www.runandgrow.com/blog/is-runkeeper-free)
- Zen Labs C25K: the paywall, lost purchases and anger at the move to subscriptions. — [AppGrooves](https://appgrooves.com/app/c25k-5k-running-trainer-pro-by-zen-labs-llc-ca/negative)
- NHS C25K: has no Apple Watch app, and Buddy runs were paused in 2026. — [motera.app](https://www.motera.app/nhs-couch-to-5k-app); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.phe.couchto5K&hl=en_GB)
- adidas: the shutdown of the adidas Training app on 31 Mar 2024 removed strength and bodyweight workouts from the adidas ecosystem. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adidas_Runtastic)
- Unique features:
  - NRC: Guided Runs with Coach Bennett and elite guests, live run sharing, weather tips, in-app race sign-up. — [Nike newsroom](https://about.nike.com/en/newsroom/releases/nike-run-club-app-new-features); [Sunrise Digest](https://thesunrisedigest.com/move/nike-run-club-review-2026/)
  - Peloton: video-based Run Analysis and IQ Pace Targets. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/22/peloton-announces-new-tread-peloton-iq-features.html)
  - Zwift: free virtual-world running and events through Companion. — [Zwift support](https://support.zwift.com/en_us/pricing-faq-HygeG0ybH7)
  - NHS: celebrity coach voices and a free, government-backed plan. — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.phe.couchto5K&hl=en_GB)
