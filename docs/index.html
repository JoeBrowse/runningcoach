What it does

The app already has all of the features below. No premium tier unlocks more features. The subscription only keeps the app working after the trial.

Muscle readiness

A body map of the muscles, front and rear. The colour of each muscle shows if it has recovered. Each muscle has its own recovery time. How hard you trained the muscle sets which time applies.

Guided workouts

Select a split, and Iron Log makes the session. It uses the exercise order that you set and prefers muscles that have recovered. You can also select each exercise yourself.

Programmes

Plans of many weeks that you follow day by day. Presets include Push/Pull/Legs and Project Arms. You can make your own plan, change the order of any day and replace exercises during a programme.

Progress charts

Estimated 1RM over time for each exercise, grouped by muscle. The app records machines and variations separately. Thus a Smith bench press does not change your barbell numbers.

Honest logging

Sets, reps and weight. You can also record reps in reserve. Supersets and drop sets. The app calculates warm-up sets from your real working weight, not from an estimate.

Two modes

Simple Mode records sets, weight and reps. Advanced Mode adds RIR, machine records, volume analysis and 1RM tests. You can change the mode at any time.

Privacy is part of the design

Many apps promise that they will not misuse your data. Iron Log has no data that it can misuse. The design of the app makes this true, not only a policy.

  • There is no Iron Log server and no Iron Log account. You do not sign up, and there is no server to attack.
  • The app writes your workouts to the storage on your device and reads them from there. It stores data in no other location.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no ads. No third-party SDKs collect data.
  • The app connects only to Google Play. It does this sometimes, to check if this account has a subscription.
  • Backup writes a plain JSON file to your Downloads folder. You can read your data in this format.
  • If you cancel the subscription, the app stops. Your history stays on your device. Backup continues to work in both cases.
