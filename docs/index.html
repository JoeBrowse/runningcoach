Skid Marks

A free running coach and gym tracker in one app

Skid Marks plans your runs around your gym days. It changes the plan when life changes, and it tells you why.

All features are free. No account, no ads, no paywall. Your data stays on your phone.

Three modes, one week

Use the switch at the top of the screen. Each session goes into one log, so you never log a session twice.

Plan

Your master week. Run days and gym days are in one plan. Today's session, the week, a weekly review, races and goals.

Run

The running coach. GPS recording with voice cues, warm-ups, live pace guidance, progress, zones and race prediction.

Lift

The gym tracker, based on Iron Log. Log sets, follow programmes, see 1RM progress and which muscles have recovered.

What makes it different

Gym days count

A heavy leg day counts as a hard day. The plan keeps it away from quality runs and the long run. Before a race, lifting changes to maintenance.

Honest safety rules

Weekly distance grows by 10% or less. Hard days never touch. About 80% of running is easy. The app checks the real plan, and tests check thousands of plans.

Changes with reasons

Missed a run, ill, in pain, on holiday or back after a break? The app asks first. Then it shows each change and its reason. You can undo any change.

Any goal

Run/walk to a first 5K, 5K to marathon, a 50K trail race, or no race at all: keep fit, faster 5K, build to 10K. A, B and C races, and recovery after each race.

Simple or Advanced

Simple mode uses plain effort words and hides scores. Advanced mode shows the fitness score, zones, predictions and the fitness, fatigue and form chart.

Bring your history

Import Strava and Garmin export files, and FIT, GPX and TCX files, many at once. Duplicates are skipped. Optional Intervals.icu link with your own key.

Privacy is part of the design

  • There is no Skid Marks server and no account.
  • No analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • The app connects to the internet only for links that you turn on: local weather (Open-Meteo) and Intervals.icu.
  • The app uses your location only while you record a run.
  • Strava data is shown only to you. The app never sends it to an AI.
  • Backups are plain JSON files that you can read.
