Privacy Policy

Skid Marks · last updated 25 November 2026

Skid Marks does not collect, send or store any personal data on a server.

You do not make an account. There is no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We have no server that receives your data. The app is free. There are no purchases.

What the app stores, and where

The app writes all the data that you enter to storage on your device. The data stays there. This includes workouts, sets, weights, reps, personal records, programmes, runs, routes, plans, races, shoes, bodyweight readings, height, age and your settings.

We cannot see any of this data. No copy of it exists in a different location.

What leaves your device

No data that you enter leaves your device.

The app makes network calls only when you turn on an optional link. Each link is off until you turn it on:

Local weather (Open-Meteo). The app sends an approximate location to Open-Meteo to get the local weather forecast. Open-Meteo is free and needs no key. The app sends no other data.

Intervals.icu. The app uses the API key that you give it to read your own activities and wellness data from your Intervals.icu account. The key stays on your device. The app does not send your Skid Marks data to Intervals.icu.

When all links are off, the app makes no network calls.

Permissions

The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts or health data. It asks for your location only when you record a run with phone GPS. The route stays on your device. It asks to save files only when you make a backup or an export.

Deleting your data

Your data is only on your device. When you uninstall Skid Marks, this removes your data. You can also delete the data at any time in the app settings of your device. We have no data to delete for you, because we never had your data.

Note: This removal is permanent. There is no cloud copy, so you cannot recover data that you remove in this way. Make a backup first.

Children

Skid Marks is not for children under 13. The app collects no data from any person, and this includes children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version at this address. We will also change the date above.
