# AI-driven / adaptive endurance coaching apps (status as of 2026-11-25)

Scope: Humango (HumanGO), Enduco, Athletica.ai, PKRS.AI, Kotcha, "Pace" apps, MOTTIV, AI Endurance, Runzy, plus platform AI coaches (WHOOP Coach, Google Health Coach (formerly Fitbit Gemini coach), Garmin Connect+ Active Intelligence, Strava Athlete Intelligence / Claude connector) and general LLMs (ChatGPT). Research budget was limited (~30 tool calls), so App Store review samples are small and Reddit coverage was thin. Where a fact is not sourced it is in Gaps.

## Q1. Does each app exist, is it active, and what does it cost (free tier, trial)?

### Takeaway
Most of the dedicated AI endurance planners cluster at about US$15-20/month or $120-200/year with a 7-14 day trial and no real free tier. Platform coaches are cheaper add-ons ($7-10/month) but do less planning. PKRS.AI, MOTTIV and Runzy look dormant: their last iOS updates were in 2023-2024. Runzy is a human-coach app, not an AI coach. "Pace" is not one product but a crowded name shared by several small apps.

- The dedicated AI planners are priced close to Runna's monthly price ($19.99) but well above its annual price ($119.99). Kotcha's annual options ($59.99-$119.99) are the cheapest annual offers among the dedicated running apps.

- GBP prices were found only for AI Endurance, Garmin, WHOOP and PACE AI. For the others, UK pricing would need an in-app check.

### Gaps
- Official GBP prices for HumanGO, Enduco, Athletica and Kotcha were not found.

- PKRS.AI's current site status was not checked directly. Treat it as unconfirmed rather than confirmed shut down.
- I could not confirm whether MOTTIV's servers are still live; only the App Store date is known.

## Q2. Features, and how the AI actually adapts (data, model). Feature matrix

### Takeaway
"Adaptive" means very different things across these apps:
- **AI Endurance**: a per-user neural network (a "digital twin"), retrained every 24 hours.
- **Athletica**: a modified Banister impulse-response model plus HRV/recovery inputs.
- **Kotcha**: a weekly LLM-agent review every Sunday.
- **HumanGO**: continuous re-planning from workouts, HR, sleep, fatigue and schedule.
- **Enduco**: re-planning when days are blocked and from daily form and feedback.

Platform coaches (WHOOP, Google, Garmin, Strava) mainly interpret data or suggest single workouts. Only Google builds a weekly fitness plan.

### Cited Findings

**How adaptation works (per app)**
- **AI Endurance**:
  - Its "Digital Twin" is a neural network retrained every 24 h on the athlete's workouts. It searches candidate plans for the best predicted race-day result. New athletes borrow data from similar athletes, and the plan search originally stayed within one standard deviation of historical volume.
  - Race prediction claims about 5% accuracy with 100+ GPS runs or power rides; the review found no published validation.
  - Uses DFA alpha 1 thresholds (needs a chest strap). The recovery model uses HRV, RHR, DFA a1, load, morning check-ins and soreness. A durability metric is still unpeer-reviewed. Critical Speed was added Oct 2026.
  - LLM chat: Gemini 3.7 Flash by default since Sept 2026, with Claude Sonnet 5.5 as an option. Chat can create and reschedule workouts, adjust zones and replan weeks. There is also a ChatGPT app and an MCP server for ChatGPT and Claude.
  - Source: [SensAI review, Oct 2026 — competitor-authored](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/ai-endurance-review-2026).
- **Athletica.ai**:
  - A baseline fitness test sets zones, which update as you progress. Workouts adjust to performance and recovery.
  - Sports: triathlon, duathlon, running, cycling, rowing and HYROX.
  - The AI Coach "analyzes your training data and explains it" but does not change the plan without user input. Integrations: Garmin, Strava, COROS, Wahoo, Concept2, plus Intervals.icu — [athletica.ai/pricing](https://athletica.ai/pricing).
  - The company says the system is "based on modified Bannister model, absolutely not a black box". It says Athletica does not do race prediction and takes a conservative approach to volume — [Athletica forum, May 2026](https://forum.athletica.ai/t/athletica-ai-review-after-marathon-training-block-two-thumbs-down/4904).
- **HumanGO**:
  - Its AI coach "Hugo" adapts plans from workouts, HR, sleep, fatigue and schedule.
  - Sports: running 5K-marathon, cycling, triathlon from sprint to full Ironman, swim, strength, plus longevity and six new sports.
  - Integrations: Garmin, Apple Watch, Suunto, Strava, Polar.
  - Extras: injury-prevention work, "Tribes" community groups, and a Coach Mode for multi-athlete management.
  - Source: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1554430755).
- **Enduco**:
  - Plans adapt for missed sessions or illness, updated "24/7", and account for daily fitness and feedback.
  - Covers running, cycling, swimming and triathlon, with strength and mobility, and supports multiple competitions.
  - AI Coach Chat is capped at 10 messages a week on Pro and 100 a week on Pro+.
  - Exports planned workouts to wearables. Named integrations: Garmin, Wahoo, COROS, Polar, Suunto, Strava, Fitbit, TrainingPeaks, Zwift, Rouvy, Intervals.icu, Hammerhead. Swim workouts go to Apple Watch, and Apple Health is imported.
  - It used to have free route-finding (mentioned in a 2020 review).
  - Sources: [enduco.app](https://enduco.app), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1483216578).
- **Kotcha**:
  - Four AI agents: Head Coach, Nutritionist, Data Analyst and Personal Trainer, trained on NN Running Team methods — [Athletics Illustrated](https://athleticsillustrated.com/eliud-kipchoge-introduces-kotcha-the-next-generation-of-ai-running-coaching/). A virtual Physiotherapist covers injury-risk signals — [Kotcha help center](https://intercom.help/kotcha/en/articles/11970203-what-is-kotcha).
  - Every Sunday it reviews data and feedback to design the next week, and missed runs feed into the following week. It debriefs after every run and offers chat on pacing, fuel and soreness.
  - Plans cover 5K to marathon, trail and general fitness. It includes strength and conditioning with exercise videos and a race-day nutrition strategy using Maurten products.
  - Syncs with Apple Watch, Garmin, COROS, Huawei and Strava. Goal and B-race target paces are sent to Apple Watch (v1.23.0). It also has a community map and a Wall of Fame.
  - Source: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kotcha-running-trail/id6746164787).
- **MOTTIV**:
  - Plans from 5K to Ironman, duathlon, gran fondo and swimrun. HR, power and pace zones. Workout nutrition advice; strength, mobility and yoga; workout videos and race guidance.
  - Social feed and Groups. Integrations: Strava, Garmin Connect, TrainingPeaks, Wahoo.
  - The listing does not describe adaptive or AI features.
  - Source: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1561001166).
- **PKRS.AI**: the AI "Myra" re-optimizes the plan daily and weekly after session debriefs, alongside a team of four human coaches. Works with "ALL smartwatches" and can upload to watch or Zwift — [appwisp listing](https://appwisp.com/app/android/ai.peakersinc.peakers), [Endurance Sportswire 2020](https://endurancesportswire.com/?p=155160) (**dated**).
- **Platform coaches**:
  - **WHOOP Coach** gives daily sleep, strain and recovery recommendations plus AI answers, and now has memory to balance race prep with Healthspan — [trackervs](https://trackervs.com/pricing/whoop-pricing/), [WHOOP 2026 what's new](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/2026-whats-new/). It feeds Advanced Labs results (65 biomarkers, US) into the Weekly Plan — [Quest newsroom](https://newsroom.questdiagnostics.com/2025-09-30-WHOOP-Launches-Clinician-Reviewed-Advanced-Labs,-Unlocking-a-Comprehensive-View-of-Human-Health). WHOOP AI now works with Strength Trainer, which is focused on strength rather than running plans — [WHOOP 2026](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/2026-whats-new/).
  - **Google Health Coach (Gemini)** creates a personalised fitness plan with day-by-day workouts and lets you build custom workouts in natural language — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/google-health/google-health-coach). In a 2025 preview test it used past run history (5K runs) and added strength work — [Yahoo/Independent via search](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/gemini/articles/just-tried-google-gemini-ai-151002396.html). It saves "Coach Notes" from conversations — [Fitbit support](https://support.google.com/fitbit/answer/16961408?hl=en).
  - **Garmin Connect+ Active Intelligence** sends AI prompts at wake, after workouts and in the evening, based on sleep, HRV, load and stress. It has no AI chat. Garmin Coach adaptive plans remain free — [the5krunner Apr 2026](https://the5krunner.com/2026/04/20/garmin-connect-plus-review/).
  - **Strava Athlete Intelligence** gives post-workout summaries (pace, HR, elevation, Relative Effort) and labels effort as easy, moderate or hard — [the5krunner 2024](https://the5krunner.com/2024/10/03/strava-athlete-intelligence-subscribers/). "Instant Workouts" recommendations have been piloted since Nov 2025 — [T3](https://www.t3.com/active/strava-2026-future-and-challenges). A read-only MCP connector lets paying subscribers query their Strava data through Claude; Strava says it "is looking to support other AI clients" — [Marathon Handbook](https://marathonhandbook.com/strava-now-lets-runners-talk-to-their-training-data-through-claude/).
- **ChatGPT as coach**: experts did not rate its plans optimal, though quality improves with more input. It did not ask clarifying questions — [paper summary](https://monkemanx.github.io/paper-summary/chatgpt_generated_training_plans_for_runners/). Weekly mileage totals did not add up — [Daily Beast](https://www.thedailybeast.com/i-asked-chatgpt-for-help-with-marathon-training-and-it-almost-ruined-the-race). Reliability declined over weeks, with hallucinations after about 3 months (Bloomberg via Gigazine) — [Gigazine](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260413-training-marathon-ai-coach).

### Inferences
- Garmin rows 5, 6, 9, 18 and 19 rely on general knowledge of Garmin watches, not on sources fetched here; the report writer should treat them as background.
- AI Endurance is the most "open" of the group: it has an MCP server, a ChatGPT app and a choice of LLM. Kotcha is the most running-specific and consumer-friendly, with multi-agent personas, a pro-athlete brand and fueling.
- None of the dedicated AI planners I checked advertises audio-guided runs or its own phone GPS tracking. They rely on watches. This is a clear gap versus Runna, Nike Run Club or similar apps.

### Gaps
- I found no sources on audio cues or treadmill support for HumanGO, Enduco, Athletica, AI Endurance or Kotcha.
- Kotcha Android availability was not confirmed.
- The Google Health Coach's running-specific features (pace targets, race plans) are poorly documented.
- Apple's Workout Buddy (watchOS 26) and Samsung's Running Coach were not researched in depth. Samsung's Running Coach on Galaxy Watch 8 creates a custom plan to start running or improve — [Yahoo](https://tech.yahoo.com/wearables/articles/4-things-consider-using-googles-144810739.html).

## Q3. Top praise and top complaints (reviews)

### Takeaway
Praise centres on genuine re-planning around life and illness and on watch sync. Complaints centre on:
- opaque metrics and conservative or odd marathon-specific programming (Athletica);
- "adaptive" not adapting sensibly after a layoff (AI Endurance);
- rigidity and slow loading (Kotcha);
- thin, generic insights (Garmin Active Intelligence, Strava AI);
- LLM drift and arithmetic errors (ChatGPT).

Independent, large-sample reviews are scarce for all the small apps.

### Cited Findings
- **Athletica (May 2026 marathon-block review on its own forum)**:
  - Praise: user-friendly UI, fun interval and progression runs, a supportive AI coach, and a 2-minute marathon PR.
  - Complaints: zones recalculated too low after injury; only minimal strength work; no runs over 25 km and no marathon-pace or race-simulation runs; race-week "running form" of 3-9 against the 15-25 the app calls peak; opaque metrics. The reviewer abandoned the app in the final month and felt it was little better than free plans at 30 CAD a month.
  - Source: [Athletica forum](https://forum.athletica.ai/t/athletica-ai-review-after-marathon-training-block-two-thumbs-down/4904).

A 70.3 user praised the instant adjustments and fair pricing (per search summary of the same thread).

  - Complaints: "adaptive part not there yet", e.g. 6x3 min at VO2max as the first workout after a layoff; needs 100+ activities and a chest strap for DFA a1; no Apple Health; limited strength; no AI swim.

### Inferences
- Features runners would miss (inferred from praise): automatic re-planning around missed or sick days (HumanGO, Enduco, Kotcha), workouts landing on the watch automatically, deload weeks, and the ability to move workouts by drag-and-drop.
- The recurring weakness of algorithmic planners is marathon-specificity: long runs and marathon-pace work. Athletica in particular can be undertrained because of conservative volume caps.

### Gaps
- Reddit sentiment for HumanGO, Enduco, Athletica, AI Endurance and Kotcha was not retrieved; searches returned mostly forums and App Store pages.
- I found no DC Rainmaker reviews of these specific apps. Trustpilot results for "Humango" returned the unrelated company "Humalingo".
- Google Play ratings were not retrieved, except AI Endurance (5K+ downloads, no rating).

## Q4. Unique features others lack

### Takeaway
Each app's differentiator is narrow:
- **AI Endurance**: per-athlete neural "digital twin", DFA alpha 1, durability, MCP/ChatGPT integration and LLM choice.
- **Kotcha**: Kipchoge/Jornet brand, a multi-agent coach team and Maurten fueling plans.
- **Athletica**: a transparent, documented Banister model, plus rowing and HYROX.
- **HumanGO**: the broadest sport list, longevity plans and a coach mode.
- **Enduco**: German data hosting, a family plan and very broad device/platform integrations.
- **WHOOP**: bloodwork (Advanced Labs) feeding the coach.
- **Strava**: chat with your own data via Claude.

### Cited Findings
- AI Endurance: daily-retrained neural net searching plans for the best predicted race result; DFA a1 thresholds; durability metric; Critical Speed target; MCP server and ChatGPT app; choice of Gemini or Claude for chat — [SensAI](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/ai-endurance-review-2026).
- Kotcha: four AI personas plus a virtual physio, trained on NN Running Team methods; Maurten race-day nutrition; co-built with Kipchoge and Jornet — [Athletics Illustrated](https://athleticsillustrated.com/eliud-kipchoge-introduces-kotcha-the-next-generation-of-ai-running-coaching/), [Kotcha help](https://intercom.help/kotcha/en/articles/11970203-what-is-kotcha), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kotcha-running-trail/id6746164787).
- Athletica: modified Banister model, documented in the web app; rowing, HYROX and Concept2 support — [Athletica forum](https://forum.athletica.ai/t/athletica-ai-review-after-marathon-training-block-two-thumbs-down/4904), [athletica.ai/pricing](https://athletica.ai/pricing).
- HumanGO: longevity plans, six new sports, a loyalty program, Tribes and a Coach Mode — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1554430755).
- Enduco: Germany-hosted data; Pro Family 1-year plan ($199.99); planning for multiple competitions — [enduco.app](https://enduco.app), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1483216578).
- WHOOP: Advanced Labs (65 biomarkers via Quest, US) feeding the Weekly Plan, and Coach memory — [Quest](https://newsroom.questdiagnostics.com/2025-09-30-WHOOP-Launches-Clinician-Reviewed-Advanced-Labs,-Unlocking-a-Comprehensive-View-of-Human-Health), [WHOOP](https://www.whoop.com/us/en/thelocker/2026-whats-new/).
- Strava: a Claude MCP connector (read-only) for all paying subscribers — [Marathon Handbook](https://marathonhandbook.com/strava-now-lets-runners-talk-to-their-training-data-through-claude/).
- Google Health Coach: natural-language custom workout creation; bundled free with Google AI Pro/Ultra — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/google-health/google-health-coach), [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/google-health-premium-price-inclusions-features-3664507/).

### Inferences
- MCP and LLM interoperability (AI Endurance, Strava→Claude) is the 2026 trend. A new app could differentiate by being the "data source" other LLMs coach from, or by combining audio-guided runs (which the AI planners lack) with true adaptive planning.
- Dormant apps (PKRS.AI, MOTTIV, Runzy) should be excluded from a "current" comparison table or flagged as legacy.

### Gaps
- The accuracy claims of AI Endurance's race predictions and digital twin have no independent validation — [SensAI](https://www.sensai.fit/blog/ai-endurance-review-2026).
- I found no independent head-to-head test of these apps' marathon outcomes.
